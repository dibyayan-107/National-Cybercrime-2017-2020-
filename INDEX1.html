Understanding India's Cyber Crime Landscape

The Cyber Crime Analytics Portal presents cyber crime incidents recorded across Indian States and Union Territories between 2017 and 2020.

The portal provides state-wise statistics, yearly trends, category analysis and interactive visual insights from the dataset.
